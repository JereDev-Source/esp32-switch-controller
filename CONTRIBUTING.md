# Contributing

Explain the change, hardware, ESP-IDF version, and console version. Keep changes
small and preserve all license and credit notices.

Do not add amiibo dumps, keys, credentials, logs, or compiled files. Document
the source, version, and license of new dependencies.

Original contributions are accepted under GPL-3.0-only. See
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) before changing third-party
code.
